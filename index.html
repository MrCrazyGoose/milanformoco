About

I've spent nearly half my life organizing for Democrats and advocating for a new generation of leaders. I'll keep showing up, as we all must, to deliver real change for our communities.

My peers and I may be the next generation of voters, but we are this generation of organizers, advocates, and change-makers. That's why we're working across this country to build a better future for all.
